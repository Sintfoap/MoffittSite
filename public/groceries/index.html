        Send it

        Kroger

        Fills your own cart at Kroger and its stores (Ralphs, Fred Meyer, King Soopers, Smith's, Fry's, Harris Teeter and more). Each line is matched to a product at your store, and what you send is picked again next time. You check out in Kroger's app.

        Instacart

        Makes an Instacart shopping list from the order. Open it, choose any store Instacart shops at, and check out there.

        Walmart

        Walmart doesn't let other apps fill a cart or place an order: its APIs are only for sellers and advertisers. Each line on the order has a Walmart link that searches for it instead.
