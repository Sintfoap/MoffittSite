Sweep

Suggestions from your chats

Sweep reads the rooms you pick and suggests events, tasks and list items for Drift. Nothing is added until you accept it, and what you accept or turn down shapes what it suggests next.

Sweep isn't set up on this site yet. The site's admin needs to add the ANTHROPIC_API_KEY secret (see the README).
You need Drift to accept suggestions. Ask the site's admin to turn it on for you.
