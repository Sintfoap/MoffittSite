    Paste a recipe's text

    Copy the text out of a photo (Live Text on an iPhone, Google Lens on Android) or a page, and paste it here. It's split into the title, ingredients and steps for you to check before saving.

    Read photos with Claude

    Without a key, photos are read by text recognition on your device, which is good with printed pages. Claude reads them better, handwriting too, with your own Anthropic API key, so nobody else is billed for yours. Make one at console.anthropic.com. It's kept sealed on the site and never shown again; Sweep uses the same one.
