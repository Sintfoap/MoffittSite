Recipes

Keep recipes from any link, photo or file, scale them, and send what to buy to Drift.

How it works

Paste a link to a recipe (any recipe site, or one shared from Recipe Keeper), snap a photo of a cookbook page or recipe card (read by the site's AI, no key needed, or by Claude with your key), paste a recipe's text, or import a whole file of them from More ways to add. Recipe Keeper's export works as its folder or .zip, pictures and all, and importing it again fills in any that are missing. Give recipes labels like Season: Fall (from the one list kept in Recipe labels) to filter and sort them, or let Suggest labels find them from each recipe's words, for you to check first. Mark the ones you've made and give them stars (just for you), then filter to old favorites or to ones you haven't tried yet. Share one on the Recipe board and Reviews lights up when someone who made it says how it turned out.

    Read photos with Claude

    Without a key, photos are read by the site's AI (Cloudflare's), or by text recognition on your device when that can't. Claude reads them better, handwriting too, with your own Anthropic API key, so nobody else is billed for yours. Make one at console.anthropic.com. It's kept sealed on the site and never shown again; Sweep uses the same one.
