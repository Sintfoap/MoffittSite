How it works

Everything is one of three kinds. Measured things (flour, sugar, oil) are kept exactly, by weight or volume. Counted things (cans, eggs, boxes) go by whole units, so using half a can still uses the can. Gauged things (spices, vanilla) go from Full to Empty, and you move them down yourself. Any of them can be a staple, something you keep stocked: you say when it's running low (2 lb, 3 cans, ½ left), and then it shows under Running low.

Each time you buy something it's kept as its own purchase, with its own expiry date, and the soonest to expire is used first. Scanning receipts and barcodes from your phone, and taking what recipes need, are coming next.
