What food you have, how much is left, and what to use soon.

How it works

Everything is one of three kinds. Staples (flour, sugar, oil) are measured exactly, and each has a low point you set. Counted things (cans, eggs, boxes) go by whole units, so using half a can still uses the can. Gauged things (spices, vanilla) go from Full to Empty, and you move them down yourself.

Each time you buy something it's kept as its own purchase, with its own expiry date, and the soonest to expire is used first. Scanning receipts and barcodes from your phone, and taking what recipes need, are coming next.
